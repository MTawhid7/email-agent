{# ── Smart Filtering ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Smart Filtering

The agent uses two layers to avoid sending unwanted replies:

  • CC / BCC detection — your connected Gmail address is auto-detected. When an email arrives and you are only CC'd or BCC'd (not the primary recipient in To:), the thread is skipped.
  • AI reasoning — Gemini analyses each email and decides whether it actually needs a reply from you (e.g. a direct question or request), or is purely informational (FYI, announcement, confirmation receipt).
{{ input_field('team_domain', 'Company Domain', value=config.get('team_domain',''), placeholder='yourcompany.com', hint='Enter your company\'s email domain (e.g. technyx.com). All senders from this domain are automatically treated as teammates — if you are only CC\'d on their emails, the agent will skip them.') }}
Teammates with personal email addresses (e.g. Gmail, Outlook personal) are not covered by the domain rule. Go to Contacts, add them, and tick the Teammate checkbox — the agent will then skip their emails when you are CC'd.
